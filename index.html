Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026

ASA Site Administrator does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind.

Everything you enter into the app — saved connection profiles, hostnames, and credentials — is stored locally on your own Mac. Passwords are stored in the macOS Keychain; all other connection details are stored in the app's local preferences. None of this data ever leaves your computer except as part of the direct SSH connection you configure to your own Avaya Communication Manager system.

The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and makes no network connections other than the SSH session you initiate yourself.

Questions about this policy can be sent to travisbarnes@yahoo.com.
